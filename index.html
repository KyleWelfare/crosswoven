    How It Works

      How to Play

      • Lay threads between the pegs so the woven cloth matches the pattern.
      • Drag from a peg to the other peg of its colour and number. The number is the thread's length in squares, both pegs included. A peg marked 1 is a whole thread on its own.
      • Each square shows the threads that pass through it. Blank squares take none.
      • Where two colours mix, their threads cross straight over each other, one across and one down.
        • Red and blue make purple
        • Red and yellow make orange
        • Blue and yellow make green
      • Drag back along a thread to shorten it. Tap a peg to clear its thread.
